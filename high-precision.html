Comparison with published experiments

Published findings, evaluation designs used here, and results measured by this project are recorded separately.

Paper and experiment Published result Scope of this project
ICML 2025, fixed-modulus LCG Fixed generator modulus with held-out parameters. Some settings reached 100% next-integer test accuracy. Literature audit. The synthetic control also fixes the history and measures next-value accuracy on held-out sequences.
ICML 2025, unseen modulus The modulus itself was held out. The scaling analysis used the history needed to reach 60% accuracy. Literature audit. The independent benchmark holds out an eight-position dependency in a different synthetic decimal task.
ICLR 2026, separate and mixed training On specified small generator variants, separate training was near perfect and mixed training exceeded 90% for each test variant. Literature audit. The app’s Lag-aware Transformer computes lag statistics from up to 1,024 recent decimal digits. Its attention model was trained on synthetic sequences, and learned parameters remain fixed during evaluation. Scores are reported separately.
ICLR 2026, truncation study The 95% result is binary next-bit prediction for a specified truncated LCG, with a 50% chance baseline. Literature audit. The synthetic report predicts ten classes, with 10% per-position chance accuracy in the uniform control.
Higher-order Markov and MTD Mixtures of lag-specific transitions estimate conditional probabilities, with results tied to model and sparsity assumptions. The app uses a simplified MTD and measures it at several history lengths on the same synthetic streams.
Recursive and direct multi-step forecasts The study compares recursive forecasting and models fitted directly for each horizon. The app uses both forecast designs and locks all five positions from the same history boundary.
